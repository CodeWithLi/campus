基于Spring Boot的高校设备管理系统设计与实现
摘    要

随着高校办学规模的不断扩大，以及高校学科发展和人才培养的需要，设备管理工作在高校中的地位与作用也越来越突出。规范化、科学化、制度化、信息化的管理是提高仪器设备的投资效率和使用效率的前提。针对目前高校设备管理中存在的问题，开发一个基于J2EE平台、B/S多层体系结构、MVC设计模式和Struts开源框架的设备管理系统是很必要的。
XXXX系统采用先进的计算机技术、网络技术开发了一个可视化的设备管理软件，使高校对设备的信息管理变得方便快捷，减少人员的工作量，节省人力，提高工作效率和服务水平；同时，也使高校设备管理工作进一步规范化、科学化、信息化。
XXXXX系统的开发是在idea开发环节下，运用当前流行的Spring框架完成的，最终实现了对高校的设备管理，包括了设备管理、设备统计、设备查询、设备报修废、安全管理等几模块，相应的完成了对设备信息的统计录入、查询、维护管理和用户权限分配等工作。

关键词： 设备管理；高校信息化；设计模式
The Design and Implementation of an Athlete Physical Fitness Monitoring System Based on STM32
Abstract

With the continuously enlarging of a school’s scale, and the need of developing scientific of college and fostering person with ability, equipment management become more and more important. Management which is standardization, scientific, institutional and informationization is a precondition to improve investment efficiency and use efficiency of equipment. Aiming at the problem existing in school management, and combining actual situation, it is indispensable to develop an equipment management system that is based on J2EE platform, B/S multi-level system structural, MVC design model and Struts open source frame.
The system uses advanced computer technology, network technology has developed a visualization device management software to enable colleges and universities of equipment information management has become convenient to reduce the workload of staff, to save manpower and improve efficiency and service levels; at the same time, but also the management of colleges and universities to further the standardization of equipment, scientific and information technology.
In the realization of this system in the Eclipse platform, the use of the popular Struts framework and Hibernate framework completed. The system is mainly to achieve management of colleges and universities, including the equipment management, equipment statistics, equipment, information, equipment repair of waste, safe management of several modules, the appropriate equipment to complete the recording of statistical information, inquiry, maintenance and management and user rights distribution and so on.

Keywords: Equipment management; University informationization; Design patterns
